# Limitations, Confounders & Audit Boundaries

## 1. Known Analytical Boundaries
1. **Dynamic Pure Oxygen Gas Blending:** While standard sparged air fails at $140\text{ h}^{-1}$, advanced facilities can blend up to $35\% \text{ O}_2$ by volume using nitrogen-diluted cryogenic liquid oxygen. However, in a methanol fermentation, off-gas oxygen concentrations must be strictly controlled to stay outside the flammability envelope ($6.7\%–36\%\text{ methanol vapor in air}$), requiring costly catalytic oxidizers or off-gas dilution.
2. **Alternative Promoters:** This audit models the standard methanol-inducible $\text{AOX1}$ promoter. Constitutive or derepressed promoters (e.g. $\text{GAP}$, $\text{PDF}$, or sorbitol/glycerol co-feeding) reduce heat and oxygen demand, but yield lower total transcription rates ($q_p \approx 0.0008\text{ g/g/h}$).
3. **Viscosity & Non-Newtonian Rheology:** At $160\text{ g/L}$ wet cell weight, *P. pastoris* broth exhibits shear-thinning pseudoplastic rheology, reducing apparent $k_L a$ by up to $30\%$ in stagnant boundary zones near vessel walls.

## 2. Confounding Factors in Pitch Materials
- **Shake-Flask Titer Reporting:** Startups frequently report titers based on total cellular lysate or concentrated supernatant without reporting initial dilution factors or non-specific BSA-equivalent Bradford assay interference.
- **Assay Specificity:** Commercial lactoferrin must be fully folded, iron-saturated or apo-conformed, and biologically active. Unfolded lactoferrin aggregates appear in total protein assays but possess zero commercial value.

## 3. Explicit Falsification Criteria
The conclusion of this study (**REJECT CLAIMED ECONOMICS**) would be falsified if:
1. The company provides third-party verified, continuous 120-hour data from a validated $50,000\text{ L}$ vessel proving an extracellular, properly folded rhLF titer $\ge 35\text{ g/L}$ with a dissolved oxygen level maintained above $20\%$ saturation.
2. An innovative micro-bubble aeration or silicone-membrane oxygenator demonstrates a volumetric $k_L a \ge 350\text{ h}^{-1}$ at $50,000\text{ L}$ scale with power input $< 4\text{ kW/m}^3$.
