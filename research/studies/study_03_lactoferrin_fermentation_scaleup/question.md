# Research Question & Diligence Specification

## 1. Primary Research Question
Does the $40.0\text{ g/L}$ commercial fermentation titer claimed in pitch materials for recombinant human lactoferrin (rhLF) in $50,000\text{ L}$ bioreactors hold up to thermodynamic, gas-liquid mass transfer ($k_L a$), and heat-exchange physics, or is it an unphysical extrapolation from benchtop shake-flask data?

## 2. Decision Framework
- **Decision Maker:** Food-Tech & Alternative Protein Growth Fund Investment Committee.
- **Alternatives Considered:**
  1. **Option A (Invest at Claimed Economics):** Accept $40\text{ g/L}$ model ($\$22/kg$ unit cost) and participate in Series B.
  2. **Option B (Re-rate Valuation at Physics Limit):** Re-underwrite economics at physical ceiling ($14\text{ g/L}$, $\$92/kg$) and negotiate $60\%$ valuation discount.
  3. **Option C (Pass / Kill):** Reject deal due to material technical misrepresentation.

## 3. Mandatory Diligence Tests
1. **Oxygen Mass Transfer:** Can standard sparged air in a $50,000\text{ L}$ vessel provide the required Oxygen Uptake Rate (OUR)?
2. **Exothermic Heat Removal:** Can cooling water at $15\text{ °C}$ remove the metabolic heat of methanol oxidation without broth boiling?
3. **Downstream Processing Recovery:** What is the actual chromatography yield when handling $120\text{ g/L}$ dry cell weight?
