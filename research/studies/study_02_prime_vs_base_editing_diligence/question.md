# Diligence Question & Target Product Profile (TPP)

## 1. Primary Research Question
For a series of pathogenic monogenic mutations in hepatic and central nervous system (CNS) indications, does the broader edit-type versatility of Prime Editing (PEmax) justify the delivery hurdle of dual-vector trans-splicing, or should capital be directed to Adenine/Cytidine Base Editors (ABE8e/BE4) with single-vector compact delivery?

## 2. Decision Framework
- **Decision Maker:** Therapeutic Biotechnology Venture Partner & Translational Scientific Advisory Board.
- **Alternatives Considered:**
  1. **Option A:** In-license Prime Editing platform for all monogenic point mutation programs.
  2. **Option B:** In-license Base Editing platform exclusively, triaging candidates to transition-only targets.
  3. **Option C (Hybrid Triage):** Base Editing for transitions in clean windows; Prime Editing for transversions and insertions via LNP delivery.

## 3. Mandatory Diligence Tests
1. **Packaging Constraint:** Does the editor plus promoter, polyA, and guide RNA fit inside the $4.7\text{ kb}$ AAV packaging limit?
2. **Editing Purity:** What is the ratio of intended precise edit to unintended indels and bystander edits?
3. **Translational Gap:** Does performance in HEK293T translate to MMR-proficient primary hepatocytes and post-mitotic cortical neurons?
