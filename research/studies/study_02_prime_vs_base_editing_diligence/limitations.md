# Limitations, Confounders & Falsification Criteria

## 1. Known Analytical Boundaries
1. **Intein Trans-Splicing Inefficiencies:** Dual-AAV approaches for PEmax rely on split inteins (e.g. *Npu* DnaE intein). Reconstitution efficiency in vivo varies between $30\%–60\%$, requiring higher total viral vector genomes ($vg/kg$) that increase host hepatotoxicity and dorsal root ganglion (DRG) toxicity risks.
2. **pegRNA Structural Decay:** Synthetic long pegRNAs ($> 140\text{ nt}$) undergo rapid cellular 3'-exonucleolytic truncation. While engineered circular or motif-protected pegRNAs (epegRNAs with tevopreQ1) improve stability, they add sequence complexity.
3. **RNA Off-Targets of Deaminases:** Cytidine and adenine deaminases (rAPOBEC1, TadA8e) can deaminate cellular mRNAs promiscuously, causing transcriptome-wide off-target edits that require protein engineering (e.g. TadA-8e V106W) to suppress.

## 2. Confounding Factors in Literature Extrapolations
- **HEK293T Overestimation:** Academic literature overwhelmingly reports editing efficiencies in HEK293T cells, which have suppressed or non-functional mismatch repair pathways, high transfection competence, and rapid cell division. Extrapolating these efficiencies to quiescent in vivo neurons ($G_0$ phase) yields severe overestimates.

## 3. Explicit Falsification Criteria
The conclusions of this study would be falsified if:
1. A miniaturized reverse transcriptase / Cas nickase fusion under $3,800\text{ bp}$ is developed that packages into a single AAV capsid while maintaining $> 30\%$ editing efficiency.
2. An in vivo LNP formulation demonstrates non-invasive, blood-brain-barrier-penetrating delivery to deep brain structures with $> 25\%$ neuronal editing without neuroinflammation.
